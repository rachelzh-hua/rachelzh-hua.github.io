About

Two threads, one goal: reproducibility

I'm a bioinformatician at USC working across genomics pipelines and interactive data delivery. My work sits at the boundary between raw computational analysis and the biologists who need to interpret it.

On the pipeline side, I build variant-calling and de novo mutation workflows for population and evolutionary genomics in marine invertebrates. On the delivery side, I package published and in-house datasets — RNA-seq, spatial, and multiplex imaging — into password-gated web portals that collaborators query directly.

Every project is built to be re-run: version-pinned tools, documented steps, and shareable outputs.

Focus areas

  • Reproducible RNA-seq & spatial-omics reanalysis
  • De novo mutation rate estimation from trios
  • Population genomics of marine invertebrates
  • Interactive reports for wet-lab collaborators
