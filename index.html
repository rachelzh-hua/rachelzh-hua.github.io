About

Computational biology, end to end

I'm a Ph.D.-trained computational biologist who builds AI-enabled, reproducible bioinformatics workflows for genomics and multi-omics research, then turns them into interactive tools that help biologists interpret their data.

I work across the full next-generation sequencing (NGS) stack, from raw reads through interpretation: spatial transcriptomics, single-cell sequencing, bulk RNA-seq, long-read genomics, variant calling, and epigenomics, with a strong emphasis on reproducibility, automation, and clear biological interpretation. I've delivered analyses across academic, biotech, and pharmaceutical projects, from raw QC through publication-quality figures.

I also bring hands-on wet-lab experience, including sequencing library preparation, RNA and DNA extraction, PCR and qPCR, gel electrophoresis, western blot, cell culture, and liquid-handling automation, along with the sample QC that goes with them. That bench fluency helps me design analyses that reflect how the data was actually generated and catch technical artifacts early.

During my Ph.D. in the Nuzhdin Lab I focused on population and evolutionary genomics in marine invertebrates, including de novo mutation-rate estimation and selection mapping.
