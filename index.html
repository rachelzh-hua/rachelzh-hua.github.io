About

Computational biology, end to end

I'm a Ph.D.-trained computational biologist who builds AI-enabled, reproducible bioinformatics workflows for genomics and multi-omics research, then turns them into interactive tools that help biologists interpret their data.

My work spans spatial transcriptomics, single-cell sequencing, long-read genomics, and epigenomics, with a strong emphasis on reproducibility, automation, and clear biological interpretation. I've delivered analyses across academic, biotech, and pharmaceutical projects, from raw QC through publication-quality figures.

I also bring hands-on wet-lab experience, including library prep, RNA and DNA extraction, PCR, western blot, and liquid-handling automation. That bench fluency helps me design analyses that reflect how the data was actually generated.

During my Ph.D. in the Nuzhdin Lab I focused on population and evolutionary genomics in marine invertebrates, including de novo mutation-rate estimation and selection mapping.
